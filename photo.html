Flowers Target Series Find the broken bone

Flowers explores photography as a practice of object making. Each of the shots consists of multiple print outs submerged in water using real flowers- photos of photos.

A series of self-portrait cyanotypes targets. Enjoyed immensely by my friends as shooting targets muddying the definitions between photo and object, what is serious and what is light.

Find the broken bone is a project from the early days of AI, when it was still pretty hallucinatory. This project highlights its unusual use of language generated out of one phrase: find the broken bone. The colors CMYK reflect the standard of printing from the web, bridging the divide between digital and physical, intention and its unchecked advance into fantasy.
